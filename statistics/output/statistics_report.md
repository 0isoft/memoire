## Recommended interpretation language

The algorithm-guided pathway appeared feasible if most participants submitted weekly forms, showed acceptable program coverage, and provided high satisfaction/acceptability scores. Reported issues should be interpreted as targets for refinement rather than as a contradiction of high global satisfaction.

Group comparisons should be interpreted cautiously due to small sample size. Effect direction and magnitude are more informative than p-values alone.
